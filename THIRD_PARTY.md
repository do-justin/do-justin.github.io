- Native default/page layouts, navigation, Tailwind CSS, Sass, and core scripts remain gem-owned.
- The local homepage layout takes its compact profile/publication structure from the references [ACAD](https://github.com/RayeRen/acad-homepage.github.io) and [Minimal Light](https://github.com/yaoyao-liu/minimal-light), without importing their code.
- `assets/css/main.scss` follows the native entry point and adds the site's small Sass partial.
- The local bibliography layout applies the reviewed author and publication display contract.
- A local asset-cache filter resolves the v1 theme-gem assets as well as site-owned assets.
